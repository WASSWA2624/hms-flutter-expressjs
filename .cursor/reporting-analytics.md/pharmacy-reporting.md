## Reporting Layers

Build **three layers**:

1. **Operational Reports** — What happened?
2. **Analytics** — Why is it happening / what trends exist?
3. **Intelligence / Alerts** — What should we do next?

**Example:**

> **Report:** Amoxicillin sold 800 units this month.
>
> **Analytics:** Sales increased 35% over the previous month.
>
> **Intelligence:** Current stock will last approximately 6 days → reorder recommended now.

That third layer is where a pharmacy management system becomes significantly more valuable than a basic POS/reporting system.
